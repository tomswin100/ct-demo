I had a look at the GreenFields supply agreement in the data room last night. It looks like GreenFields supplies around 62% of Southern Valley's fresh produce by value.

Clause 14 says Southern Valley must not assign, novate, transfer, or undergo a "change in control" without GreenFields' prior written consent. It also says consent must not be unreasonably withheld, but GreenFields can require evidence of financial standing and operational capacity.

This seems important. If we buy the shares, there is a change in control. If we do an asset deal, we probably need assignment or novation anyway.

Tom says GreenFields will be fine with Harbour Foods as owner because we are larger and more stable. That may be true, but I would rather not rely on a handshake.

James, can you please include this in the memo as a key condition precedent / diligence item?
