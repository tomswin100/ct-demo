Hi James,

I have completed a first-pass privacy review from the materials provided.

Key points:
- Southern Valley holds names, addresses, phone numbers, payment tokens, dietary preferences, delivery instructions, and customer service notes.
- Some delivery notes include sensitive details customers typed manually, e.g. "leave at back door, baby sleeping", "elderly parent at home", and health/allergy information.
- The privacy policy is short and has not been updated since 2021.
- It says customer information is used for "order fulfilment and marketing of related products", but it does not clearly explain data sharing in an acquisition context.
- The January 2026 billing bug does not appear to have involved unauthorised access, but the records are thin.
- I cannot see a formal privacy breach register or incident response plan.

For the board memo, I would flag that the customer database is an asset but also a diligence focus. We should request:
1. incident logs;
2. complaints/refund records from the billing bug;
3. vendor contracts with Stripe, Klaviyo, and hosting providers;
4. data retention/deletion policy;
5. any privacy impact assessments; and
6. confirmation whether customer consent or updated notices are needed before post-completion data integration.
