Hi team,

I reviewed the food safety documents in the data room.

There is a registered food control plan and audit reports for 2024 and 2025. The 2025 audit report notes three corrective actions:
1. temperature logs for chilled storage were incomplete on several days;
2. allergen labelling checks were not consistently signed off;
3. one supplier certificate was expired.

The report says all corrective actions were closed by 15 February 2026. I have not yet seen supporting evidence for closure, just the target's summary.

There was also a small voluntary recall in August 2024 for a mislabelled sesame allergen in a salad dressing sachet. The recall looks contained, but we should ask for:
- recall notices;
- regulator correspondence;
- customer complaints;
- insurance notifications; and
- evidence that labelling controls changed afterwards.

From a commercial perspective, I do not think this kills the deal, but it should definitely be in the due diligence request list and warranties.
